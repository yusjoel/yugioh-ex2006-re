build_equip_zone_bitmap_for_zone11 @ 0x0807067c

Iterates over all zone entries on the current player side, checks the occupancy
state of each zone, and constructs a zone occupancy bitmap mask. After the loop,
calls forward_equip_bitmap_update_zone11(slot, bitmap) to forward the bitmap
update to the zone-11 handler. Returns 0.

Constants:
  ZONE_TABLE_BASE           = 0x0201c510
  ZONE_TABLE_PLAYER_STRIDE  = 0x868
  ZONE_ENTRY_SIZE           = 0x14

Parameters:
  r0: u8* slot_ptr -- equip effect-slot pointer
Return: r0 = 0 (fixed)

Side effects:
  Calls forward_equip_bitmap_update_zone11 (updates zone-11 equip bitmap in
  EWRAM zone state).
