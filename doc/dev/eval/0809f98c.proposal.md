## plate comment (中文, ASCII 标点)
读取 gP1LifePoints+0x1ce8 与 gP1LifePoints+0x1ce8+0x38 两个字段并 XOR; 若结果与 r0 (player_side) 不一致则返回 0.
计算 combined_slot (r1+r2), 若 > 10 (0xa) 返回 0.
调用 get_slot_effect_card_value(r0=player_side, r1=combined_slot) 检查该槽是否存在有效效果卡值.
若返回 0 则返回 0 (无效).
否则返回 0x800 (有效).
通过 .word 0x0809f98d 函数指针在 dispatch_duel_field_ai_phase_by_state_code 的 advance_display_slot_if_zone_active 路径中被调用.
Constants: gP1LifePoints=0x0201c4e0, ACTIVE_PLAYER_OFFSET=0x1ce8, XOR_OFFSET=0x38, MAX_SLOT=10, ELIGIBLE_FLAG=0x800.
