Called by banlist scan loop (0x080ef000, 0x080ef210). Checks whether two card_ids (r0=target, r1=candidate) are compatible/matching under banlist rules. Applies symmetric BST mapping to both r0 (saved as r2) and r1: returns 1 if r2==r1 directly; returns 1 if both fall within corresponding mapped ranges; otherwise returns 0. Pure leaf function, no side effects.

Inputs: r0=u16 card_id_target [0..0x1fff], r1=u16 card_id_candidate [0..0x1fff].
Outputs: r0=u32 bool (1=compatible/match, 0=not).
Side effects: none.
Constants: CARD_BOUNDARY_A=0x0fc9, CARD_BOUNDARY_B=0x1303, CARD_BOUNDARY_C=0x142d, RANGE_OFFSET_A=0xfffff05a (=-0xfa6, used for range check).
