Compact equip slot eligibility predicate: set_code dedup + [+8] valid flag only. Guards: (1) r2<=4 -- zone>4 rejects; (2) gDuelFieldSlots[r1&1][r2] bits[18:0] non-zero; (3) ldrh [slot,#8]!=0 (equip valid flag); (4) extract set_code_a=(bits[29:22]<<1)|bit[18] vs equipping slot[+4] bits[14:8] -- equal rejects. Returns 1 on pass. Shares structure with 0x08051b9c but adds [+8] check and omits check_slot_card_is_equip_whitelist and check_card_equip_eligible_for_slot. No bl calls. Read-only predicate, no side effects.
Inputs: r0=ptr equipping-card slot, r1=u8 player_id [0..1], r2=u8 zone_slot_idx [0..4].
Outputs: r0=u32 bool (1=eligible, 0=rejected).
Side effects: none.
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868.
