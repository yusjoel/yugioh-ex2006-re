Function: Equip slot eligibility check requiring owner_bit different from equip_card.side[bit0] (beq fail = same-side reject). Flow: (1) if card.side == owner_bit => fail; (2) slot_idx [0..4] and calls check_slot_card_is_equip_whitelist; whitelist miss proceeds to zone check; (3) zone chain_field nonzero; (4) check_zone_slot_equip_prerequisites. Returns 1 if all pass.

Params: r0=u8* card_ptr (equip card; [+0x2] bit0=side); r1=u8 owner_bit [0..1]; r2=u8 slot_idx [0..4]
Returns: r0=u32 eligible (0=ineligible, 1=eligible)
Side effects: none (pure query)
Constants: SLOT_IDX_MAX=4, ZONE_BASE=0x0201c510, STRIDE=0x868
