## plate comment (中文, ASCII 标点)
根据装备激活状态分派 LP 精灵或显示操作. 从 card_entry byte[+2] bit0 提取 player_id (spilled to r5). 读 DWORD_08056738 (=0x0201e2a0) [player_id*4+8] 获取 activation_state. (1) activation_state==1: 比较 LP 差值 -- 读 gP1LifePoints 两个偏移字段相加得 combined_lp; 读 [0x0201e2a0 + player_id*0x868 + 0x3d0] 得参考值; combined_lp + 0x3e8 (=1000) > 参考值则返回 1; 否则调用 enqueue_sprite_attr_clamped(player_id, 0xfa*8=0x7d0) 入队精灵并返回 1. (2) activation_state!=1 但 [0x0201b290+0x4ac]==0: 调用 invoke_card_display_op_0x31_sub1(0x1d), 步进状态计数器后返回 0. (3) [0x4ac]==1 且 LP zone 有效: enqueue_sprite_attr_clamped(player_id, 0xfa*8=0x7d0) 返回 1. (4) [0x4ac]>=2: 返回 0. 装备激活链中 LP 显示精灵触发决策点.

- ACTIVATION_STATE_BASE = 0x0201e2a0
- PLAYER_BLOCK = 0x868
- LP_BONUS = 0x3e8 (= 1000)
- SPRITE_VAL = 0x7d0 (= 0xfa << 3 = 2000)
