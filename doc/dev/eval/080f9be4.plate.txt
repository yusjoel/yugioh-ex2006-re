write_puzzle_charset_flag
Writes the low 3 bits of r0 into the low 3 bits of EWRAM [0x02000000+0x6c2c], updating the puzzle workspace charset/encoding flag byte. bit[1:0]=charset_id (0=OCG, other=non-OCG), bit2=auxiliary flag. Clears low 3 bits first using 0x8 (NOT mask), then ORs in new value. Called by scene init functions such as FUN_080f46dc (0x080f46dc, tags: [dma;sys;display]), FUN_080f4d90 (0x080f4d90, tags: [frame_counter;prng;settings]) during puzzle/settings load with r0=0 or r0=1, and FUN_080fb134 (0x080fb134) before rendering to refresh charset. Side effect: [0x02000000+0x6c2c] bits[2:0] := r0&7.
