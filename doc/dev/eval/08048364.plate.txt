Duel field slot OAM sprite render with Dark Magician of Chaos equip eligibility check. Called from 0x08059008 (duel_field). Reads gDuelFieldSlots bits[12:0] for card presence; calls count_field_copies_of_card(BANISHER=0x1332); r8 holds slot card_id (ldr+lsls+lsrs bits[12:0]); if r8==0x16f8 (DMoC) calls check_card_equip_eligibility_in_field. Builds OAM attr_word and calls enqueue_sprite_attr_record; if equip_flag calls scan_field_slots_for_equip_sprite etc. Returns 1/0. Side effects: OAM attr buffer. Constants: BANISHER_ID=0x1332, DMoC_ID=0x16f8, ATTR0=0x45/0x8045.
