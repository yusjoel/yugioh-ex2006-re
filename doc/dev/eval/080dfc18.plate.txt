Initializes the pack card list scene display. Sequentially writes BG0CNT=0x1f02 / BG1CNT=0x1c07 / BG2CNT=0x5d89 to configure three BG plane control registers; calls reset_all_bg_scroll_regs_and_shadows to zero all scroll registers; calls decompress_pack_scene_gfx to decompress and load scene OBJ/BG graphics and palette; calls render_pack_card_list_screen to render the full card list interface; calls load_pack_palette_by_slot for slots 0/1/2; calls copy_card_list_tile_rows_to_vram / render_pack_card_atk_rows_to_bg / render_pack_card_list_text_header / render_pack_info_to_vram to complete remaining elements; finally calls apply_blend_fadeout_flat to init blend and returns fixed 1. Called by FUN_080e0b3c (pack scene step dispatcher) as step 0.
