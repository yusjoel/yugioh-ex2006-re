LP display row type 8 dual-value wrapper: truncates r1/r2 to low 16 bits, calls set_lp_display_row_fields(r0, 8, r1&0xffff, r2&0xffff) with fixed type=8. Symmetric with set_lp_display_row_type5/type6/type9. Called by multiple LP cost/state update paths (0x0805658c, 0x0806a7b4, 0x0805720a etc, tags=[duel_field]).
Side effects: writes LP display row type=8 fields via set_lp_display_row_fields.
Constants: ROW_TYPE=8 (fixed; movs r1,#0x8).
