One-time system hardware and driver initialization. Three DMA3 transfers: first two copy from r0/r1, third copies from ROM(0x080000fc) to gPrng+4. Zero-fills gPrng region; initializes SRAM stub; writes IWRAM fn-ptr slots(0x03000000+0..0x3c, including frame driver and audio entry points). Configures interrupts: IE=0x2601(VBlank+Timer2+Serial), DISPSTAT=0x18, WAITCNT=0x4014; writes IRQ handler ptr. Clears input ring buffer; checks BIOS checksum writes parity bit; initializes audio engine. Sets init_complete flag. Uniquely called by crt0(0x080000c0) at game startup.
