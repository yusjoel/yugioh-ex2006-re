Builds equip chain entry for a special zone slot, handling find_equip_chain_pair cross-field lookup. r0=card_slot_ptr, r1=player_id [0..1], r2=zone_slot_idx [5..10]. Validates r2-5 in [0..5]; checks gDuelFieldSlots slot card bits[19:0] non-zero; calls check_slot_card_eligible_for_special_action(r10, r6) to verify special action eligibility; if valid: calls find_equip_chain_pair_across_field(r10, r6) to get matched pair; if result != 0xffff: double-loops player [0..1] x slot [0..4], constructs combined_key=(player<<8|slot), skips key==pair_result, calls check_zone_slot_equip_prerequisites + build_equip_chain_slot_entry. Returns 1 on success, 0 on fail. Sub-case E exit. Symmetric sibling of build_equip_chain_for_monster_zone (0x08051670), differs in slot range [5..10] and find method.

Params: r0=ptr card_slot (equip slot pointer, saved to r10); r1=u32 player_id [0..1] (saved to r9); r2=u32 zone_slot_idx [5..10] (special zone, r6=r2)
Returns: r0=u32 bool (1=chain entry built, 0=fail; Sub-case E: pop {r1}; bx r1)
Side effects: via build_equip_chain_slot_entry: equip chain entry written
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510; PLAYER_STRIDE=0x868; SLOT_STRIDE=0x14; SPECIAL_ZONE_MIN=5 (zone_slot_idx [5..10]); PAIR_NOT_FOUND=0xffff
