enqueue_lp_display_row_type2_for_card_player @ 0x0805756c

Extracts player_id from equip card entry and enqueues LP display row with row=0xffff mode=2.
Reads card_entry byte[+2] bit0 -> player_id (r0);
calls set_lp_display_row_type8(player_id, 0xffff, 2).
Returns 1 always.
mode=2 variant of enqueue_lp_display_row_clear_for_card_player (0x080567a0, mode=1);
LP display row clear in second phase of equip activation chain.

Constants:
- LP_ROW_CLEAR = 0xffff
- LP_ROW_MODE = 2

Inputs: r0=equip_card_entry ptr (byte[+2] bit0=player_id [0..1])
Output: r0 = 1 (always success)
Side effects: LP display row queue -- set_lp_display_row_type8(player_id, 0xffff, 2)
