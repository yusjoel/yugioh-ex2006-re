等待 invoke_effect_node_handler_2arg 成功后驱动状态机 5 步转换的帧驱动函数.
以 IWRAM[0x0201b290+0x4a0] (STATE_OFFSET=0x94*8) 的状态码路由: 0x80 -> 调用 invoke_effect_node_handler_2arg 测试节点; 成功则初始化激活显示并进入 0x7f; 0x7f -> check_activation_display_state_is_confirmed, 确认后读 gP1LifePoints+0x1d70 取两侧 LP/zone 表条目, 组合 tile_col*2+flip_bit 调用 enqueue_sprite_attr_with_type_select, 进入 0x7e; 0x7e -> 读 effect_slot_entry[byte2] 取 player_id 和 slot_idx, 调用 set_lp_row_type18_with_value 写 LP 行显示, 进入 0x7d; 0x7d -> 读 gP1LifePoints+0x1da8 zone 列表, 检查 [+0]=1 则调用 select_equip_target_slot_by_card_id 否则 dispatch_equip_zone_sprite_banisher_by_field_count, 进入 0x0. 副作用: 更新 LP 行类型 18 显示字段, 写 sprite 属性队列, 初始化激活显示上下文.

Constants:
- IWRAM_BASE = 0x0201b290
- STATE_OFFSET = 0x94*8 = 0x4a0
- COUNTER_OFFSET = 0x4a4
- ZONE_LIST_OFFSET = 0x1da8 (gP1LifePoints+0x1da8)
- LP_DATA_OFFSET = 0x1d70 (gP1LifePoints+0x1d70)
- PLAYER_STRIDE = 0x868
- ZONE_TABLE_BASE = 0x0201e2a0
