Duel puzzle scene VRAM initialization. Writes gPrng+0x174:=1 (scene active flag), writes DISPCNT-related reg (0x80<<0x13=0x4000000):=4 (BG mode), calls reset_display_and_obj_vram, calls store_ewram_ctx_ptr_and_clear_mode_flags(0x02029eb0) to bind duel_puzzle EWRAM context. Writes BG2CNT (0x0400000c):=1. Loads BG tile/map data from ROM: copy_bg_tile_data_to_vram(0x09dae9a4), zero_fill(0x06014000, 0x4000 halfwords), multiple copy_bytes_by_halfword and tile_2d_row_copy calls for palette/tile VRAM. indeg=0, no caller.
