副作用: qsort 修改 sp 临时缓冲区; copy_bytes_by_halfword 写 [0x030001fc+entry*8] (OAM mirror 区, 6 bytes/entry).
Constants: gPrng_base_offset=0xde*2=0x1bc, count_byte_offset=0x400, entry_copy_size=9, qsort_entry_size=0xc, oam_target=0x030001fc, write_bytes=6, sp_frame_size=0x600.
