Rounds r0 up to the nearest multiple of 10.
Formula: (r0 + 5) / 10 * 10
The +5 bias maps:  x in [0..4] -> 0,  x in [5..14] -> 10, etc.
Typical use: quantizing pixel coordinates or character widths to 10-pixel tile units.

r0 = s32 input_value (pixel coordinate or character width)
Returns: r0 = round_nearest_10(input_value) (exit via pop{r1};bx r1)

Constants:
  DECADE_BIAS = 0x05       (bias for round-to-nearest-10)
  DIVISOR = 0x0a           (divide by 10)
  MULTIPLIER_SHIFT: q*5 = q<<2+q, then lsls #1 -> q*10

Side effects: none
