Function: Scans equip chain list (base 0x0201b290+0x4cc) for a node whose type byte ([+0]) & 0xFF minus 8 falls in [0..1]; for matching nodes verifies owner_bit != card.side[bit0] (beq fail = same-side reject), slot_idx [0..4] in range, bits[21:20] (equip_field two bits) equals 0x1 (0xC0 mask, cmp 0x40); on hit calls check_zone_slot_equip_prerequisites to confirm field prerequisites. Returns 1 if all conditions met.

Params: r0=u8* card_ptr (equip card; [+0x2] bit0=side); r1=u8 owner_bit [0..1]; r2=u8 slot_idx [0..4]
Returns: r0=u32 eligible (0=ineligible, 1=eligible)
Side effects: none (pure query)
Constants: CHAIN_LIST_BASE=0x0201b290, OFFSET_COUNT=0x4cc, OFFSET_ENTRY_ARR=0x4d4, OFFSET_CARD_ARR=0x4f4, SLOT_IDX_MAX=4, TYPE_MIN=0x8, TYPE_MAX=0x9, EQUIP_BIT=0x40 (bit6 of byte[+1])
