Function: Structurally symmetric to apply_slot_equip_activation_by_zone_slot_match (0x080688dc).
Entry: r0=effect_slot_ptr. Extracts player_id (bit0) and slot_type (bits[6:2]) from [r0+0x2].
Locates corresponding word in zone slot array (base=0x0201c510, stride=0x868),
compares card_type aggregate with target count at [r6+0x4]; returns void if mismatch.
On match: checks [zone_slot+0x8] LP presence; on pass calls
apply_slot_equip_activation_with_eligibility_check(slot_ptr, player_id, slot_type, sp[0]=lp_count, 0).
Difference from 0x080688dc: sp[0] is loaded from [zone_slot+0x8] (LP presence count)
rather than fixed value 0x1.
Side effects: Triggers equip activation logic via apply_slot_equip_activation_with_eligibility_check.
Constants:
  BASE_STRIDE=0x868
  BASE_ADDR=0x0201c510
