## plate comment (中文, ASCII 标点)
按卡片类型 (field6) 和格状态, 为指定玩家格 (player_side=r0, slot_idx=r1) 生成并入队 OAM sprite attr 记录. 输入包括 5 个参数: r0=player_side, r1=slot_idx, r2=slot_base_offset (来自 sp[0x30]), r3=extra_flag, sp[0]=has_equip_head. 函数体: (1) 读取格 card_id (lsls#0x13 提取低 13 位); (2) 按 field6: 0x17=field_spell 走 check_slot_fieldspell_eligible_by_side; 0x16=equip/trap 走 query_slot_effect_eligibility_nonzero; (3) 合成 OAM attr word (bit9=player_side, bit[13:10]=slot_idx, bit14=equip_head, bit15=extra_flag, bits[8:0]+high_bits=slot_id); (4) 调用 enqueue_sprite_attr_record; (5) slot_idx<=4 且条件满足时写 strh [slot+0x8]=1 并调用 apply_equip_activation_with_id_lookup + submit_lp_bar_sprite_row_by_type. 副作用: OAM attr buffer (enqueue_sprite_attr_record); 条件写 strh [gP1LifePoints+slot_offset+0x8]=1; LP bar OAM (submit_lp_bar_sprite_row_by_type).
