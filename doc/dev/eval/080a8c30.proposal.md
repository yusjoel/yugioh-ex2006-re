# Naming Proposal: 0x080a8c30

## 提案
- **proposed_name**: dispatch_equip_phase_by_card_id_range
- **confidence**: high

## plate comment (中文, ASCII 标点)
equip 阶段 hub 分派器, 被 FUN_080ab200 (equip 子条目主控) 在 struct[0x12] bit0=1 路径
中调用 (indeg=1). 从结构体 0x0201e4d0[0x0] 读取 dword, 提取 bits[16:8] 为 card_id
(lsls #9; lsrs #17). 将 card_id 与一系列阈值常量比较 (0x17c2, 0x1487, 0x1299, 0x0fe9,
0x112e, 0x128c, 0x13b5, 0x19f8 等), 根据范围分派到不同的 equip tick 函数:
- card_id == 0x17c2 或相关: bl tick_equip_target_phase_with_lp_confirm_slot_context
- card_id 在特定范围: bl tick_equip_target_phase_with_bitmap_confirm
- card_id 范围含 0x1487-0x4: bl FUN_080a71e4 (tick_equip_phase_by_lp_combined_index)
- card_id 范围: bl FUN_080a6e74 / bl FUN_080a7b78 / bl FUN_080a87ec (normal-summon 类型)
- card_id 落入 "unknown normal summon" 段: bl check_card_id_is_normal_summon_type, 若
  返回非零则 bl FUN_080a87ec
- 其余: bl dispatch_equip_confirm_action_by_phase / dispatch_equip_phase_unless_activation_pending 等
出口 LAB_080a8fb0 汇聚; 失败出口 LAB_080a8f18 直接到父函数共享出口.
Constants: STRUCT_BASE=0x0201e4d0, card_id_field=bits[16:8] of [+0x0],
比较常量(阈值集):
  0x17c2 (Blue-Eyes Shining Dragon),
  0x1487 (Garuda the Wind Spirit),
  0x1299 (The Fiend Megacyber),
  0x0fe9 (Perfectly Ultimate Great Moth),
  0x112e (Metalzoa),
  0x128c (Red-Eyes Black Metal Dragon),
  0x13b5 (Moisture Creature),
  0x19f8 (Helios Tris Megiste).
