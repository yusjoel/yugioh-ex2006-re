Pack card-select scene slot init step function. Dispatched indirectly by dispatch_pack_card_select_substep (0x080e0fb8) via ROM step-table 0x09e495f4. When bit1=1 computes OAM tile attr/xy and writes pack_ui_state+0x58, or iterates 7 overlay sub-slots setting random anim rate and Y-offset. When bit0=1 calls update_pack_slot_oam_attr_pair. Finishes by calling tick_pack_slot_cover_oam/render_pack_slot_brightness_blend/render_pack_slot_cover_oam_sprite/tick_pack_oam_slots_and_overlay. Side-effects: pack_ui_state+0x58 OAM/anim fields, +0x100+slot*0x14 overlay sub-slot strh, +0x4 step ID, gPrng+0x1c0 bit-field clear. Returns r0=r10=r6 (0=continue/1=done).
