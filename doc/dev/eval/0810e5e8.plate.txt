Single-instruction function pointer trampoline: executes bx r8 to jump to the target function held in r8. r8 is a high non-APCS register; callers load it via .hword 0x4680 (mov r8,r0) or similar instructions. Called from 5 sites including equip zone bitmap construction (build_equip_zone_bitmap_for_player, 0x080906cc) and IWRAM init cluster (0x08111398, _fwalk). ASM comment labels this as "invoker thunk (r0=player_id, r1=side, r2=slot)".
