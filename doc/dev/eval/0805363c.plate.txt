Equip slot eligibility check with prerequisites only (no whitelist). Guards: (1) slot[+2] bit0 != r1 (opposite-side, bne fail); (2) zone [0..4]; (3) gDuelFieldSlots slot occupied; (4) slot[+8] == 0; (5) check_zone_slot_equip_prerequisites. Compared to check_equip_slot_eligible_with_whitelist_prereqs_and_eligible: omits whitelist and eligible calls. Returns 1 if all pass.

Params: r0=u8* equipping_slot (ptr); r1=u8 player_id [0..1]; r2=u8 zone_slot_idx [0..4]
Returns: r0=u32 bool (1=eligible, 0=rejected)
Side effects: none
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868, SLOT_IDX_MAX=4
