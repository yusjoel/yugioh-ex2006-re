Guards: (1) gDuelFieldSlots slot occupied; (2) zone [0..4]; (3) slot[+8] != 0; (4) check_zone_slot_equip_prerequisites; (5) get_slot_field5_score(r1,r2) <= 1000 (0xfa<<2). Params: r0=u8* equipping_slot (ptr); r1=u8 player_id [0..1]; r2=u8 zone_slot_idx [0..4]
