Constants:
- CARD_ID_ATOMIC_FIREFLY = 0x174e (Atomic Firefly)
- CARD_ID_KOZAKY_SDB = 0x18d7 (Kozaky's Self-Destruct Button)
- LP_DELTA = 0xfffffc18 = -1000 (fixed LP penalty)
- BIT18_SHIFT_MASK = lsls #0x0d; lsrs #0x1f (extracts bit18 as target player index)
