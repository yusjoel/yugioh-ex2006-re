Reads scene_ctx(0x02023360)+8 word, extracts bits[22:15] as sprite_state [0..255]. If sprite_state > 7 jumps to LAB_08026830 (alternate update path writing gPrng+0x202 scaler then jumping to SUB_08027c22).
