Equip card eligibility predicate with same-side requirement + set_code dedup + comprehensive equip check. Five guards: (1) read slot[+2] bit0 vs r1 -- side must match r1 (opposite rejects); (2) r2<=4; (3) gDuelFieldSlots[r1&1][r2] bits[18:0] non-zero; (4) extract set_code_a=(bits[29:22]<<1)|bit[18] vs equipping slot[+4] bits[14:8] -- equal rejects (same-set disallowed); (5) check_card_equip_eligible_for_slot(r0,r1,r2,0). Symmetric to 0x08051abc but side-match direction inverted: requires same-side vs opposite-side. Read-only predicate, no side effects.
Inputs: r0=ptr equipping-card slot, r1=u8 player_id [0..1], r2=u8 zone_slot_idx [0..0x14].
Outputs: r0=u32 bool (1=eligible, 0=rejected).
Side effects: none.
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868.
