Called by equip card target check chain (indeg=1, caller 0x0804ff0e). Accepts card_slot_ptr (r0, saved to r4), player_side (r1), slot_idx (r2). Calls find_equip_target_for_card_slot with r2=1 to locate the target slot. If result==0xffff (no target) -> sets r3=1 (allow); otherwise extracts player_bit (bit0) and zone_bits (bits[6:1]) from [r4+2] owner byte, combines them, and compares to target_id: if they match, r3=1; otherwise r3=0. Returns r0=r3 (bool).

Inputs: r0=u32* card_slot_ptr ([+2]=owner/zone fields); r1=u32 player_side [0..1]; r2=u32 slot_idx [0..4].
Outputs: r0=u32 bool (1=target absent or matches owner, 0=mismatch).
Side effects: none.
Constants: NO_TARGET=0xffff; PLAYER_BIT_MASK=0x1 (bit0 of [r4+2]); ZONE_BITS_MASK=0x7e (bits[6:1] of [r4+2]).
