Inputs: r0=player_id [0..1], r1=slot_data_ptr, r2=sub_slot_idx (int32, <0 skips to LAB_080abefc), r3=player_bit [0..1] (used for attr[0x12] bit9), sp[0x14]=copy_flag_arg.
