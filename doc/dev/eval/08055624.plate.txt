Requires slot_idx<=4 and target on opponent side (card_owner != player_id).
Computes zone address, verifies is_present bit19 and zone[+8] (ATK) nonzero.

enters loop calling dispatch_effect_handler_by_card_id(player_id, card_id, idx) for all effect nodes

an activatable effect node exists in the effect chain.
