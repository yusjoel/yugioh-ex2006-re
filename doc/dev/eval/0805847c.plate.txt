Two-step sprite update for equip card slot: (1) reads xy coord from EWRAM slot table at
[0x0201c510+player_id*0x868+slot_group*0x14+0xc], writes to card_entry[+8], calls
set_field_slot_bit_with_sprite_update(player_id, slot_group, bit=4, 0) to set field slot flag
and trigger sprite refresh; (2) calls enqueue_sprite_attr_with_xy_split(player_id, slot_group, 0)
to submit split-xy sprite attr to OAM queue.

Constants:
- SLOT_TABLE_BASE=0x0201c510
- PLAYER_STRIDE=0x868
- SLOT_ENTRY_SIZE=0x14
- XY_COORD_OFFSET=0xc
- BIT_INDEX=4
