Renders pack slot 2 overlay OAM sprite, corresponding to second row card slot position.
r0=ctx_ptr passed to render_overlay_oam_sprite_tiled.
Calls compute_pack_slot_oam_y_attr(1) to get slot 1 y-coord OAM attr, renders with fixed size w=9/h=2 tiles and ROM tile_attr 0x00020149. tile_base=0x149 = oam1 tile_base(0x140)+9 (=width).
Called by pack scene per-frame OAM render paths (pack_080e097c/pack_080e0af8).
Constants: SLOT_Y_INDEX=1, SPRITE_WH=0x00020009, SPRITE_ATTR=0x00020149.
Inputs: r0=ptr ctx_ptr. Returns: void (pop {r0}; bx r0).
Side effects: OAM entry written via render_overlay_oam_sprite_tiled (slot_y(1) coord, w=9 h=2).
