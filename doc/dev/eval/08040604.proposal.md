## plate comment (中文, ASCII 标点)

Constants: card_display_state_base=0x0201bcc0 (DAT_0804062c), counter_offset=0x810 (0x81*0x10=0x810), limit_offset=0x2, reset_offset=0x80c (DAT_08040630=0x80c).
