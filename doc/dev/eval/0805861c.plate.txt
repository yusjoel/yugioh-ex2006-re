Extracts player_id=[+2].bit0 and card_id=[+0]u16 from card_entry r0, calls
enqueue_sprite_attr_type11(player_id, card_id, mode=1, extra=0) to write simplified
type11 attr record into OAM sprite queue. Symmetric sibling of 0x0805845c
(enqueue_sprite_type11_for_equip_slot); difference: r3 fixed=0, no slot_field_bits extraction.
Exit: pop{r1}; bx r1 void return.

Constants:
- ATTR_MODE=1
- EXTRA_FLAG=0
