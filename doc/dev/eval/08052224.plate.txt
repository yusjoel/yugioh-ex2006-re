Large equip slot eligibility check dispatching on equipping card_id (slot[+0]). Preamble: gDuelFieldSlots[r1&1][r2] occupation check, check_slot_card_can_be_equipped, count_available_monster_slots. Then reads equipping card ID (slot[+0] bits[18:0]) and BST-dispatches to special logic. Card-id branches: Insect Imitation (0x140b)->state_code==0xa; Gearfried (0x13c3)/Metamorphosis (0x15a3)->eval_equip_chain_score + dispatch_effect_handler; Multiplication of Ants (0x13fc)->state_code==0xa; Ultra Evolution Pill (0x1715)/Spiritual Earth Art (0x1927)->state_code check; Dedication through Light and Darkness (0x1713)->check_slot_zone_bit_eligible(5); Photon Generator Unit (0x19b1)/Attack Reflector Unit (0x19b5)->check_slot_card_pair_allowed; Release Restraint (0x187e)->check_card_pair_allowed; Chu-Ske (0x185a)->range [0x185a..0x18b8]. Default (no match) returns 1. No external writes.
Inputs: r0=ptr equipping-card slot, r1=u8 player_id [0..1], r2=u8 zone_slot_idx [0..0x14].
Outputs: r0=u32 bool (1=eligible, 0=rejected).
Side effects: none.
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868, CARD_ID_Insect_Imitation=0x140b, CARD_ID_Gearfried=0x13c3, CARD_ID_Metamorphosis=0x15a3, CARD_ID_Multiplication_of_Ants=0x13fc, CARD_ID_Ultra_Evolution_Pill=0x1715, CARD_ID_Dedication_through_Light_and_Darkness=0x1713, CARD_ID_Photon_Generator_Unit=0x19b1, CARD_ID_Attack_Reflector_Unit=0x19b5, CARD_ID_Release_Restraint=0x187e, CARD_ID_Chu_Ske=0x185a.
