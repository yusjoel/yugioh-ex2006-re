Calls update_equip_target_bitmap_for_field(player_id, slot_bit, zone_flags, side_flags=2). Returns 1 if bitmap & slot_bit != 0, else 0.

Params: r0=u8 player_id [0..1]; r1=u8 player_ref [0..1]; r2=u8 slot_idx [0..9]
