Computes 64-bit signed product and returns the middle 32 bits (result >> 12).
Entry: asrs r3,r2,#0x1f sign-extends r2 to 64-bit pair (r2,r3).
Calls __muldi3(r0,r1, r2,r3) for 64-bit multiply.
Extracts result: r0 = (r1 << 0x14) | (r0 >> 0x0c) = 64-bit product right-shifted by 12
and truncated to 32 bits.
Used by upper-level NNS G2D animation code (indeg=0, fn-ptr or inlined call).

Constants:
  SIGN_EXT = asrs r3,r2,#0x1f  (sign-extends r2 to r2:r3 64-bit pair)
  SHIFT_HI = 0x14 = 20          (high 32-bit left shift amount)
  SHIFT_LO = 0x0c = 12          (low 32-bit right shift amount)
  NET_SHIFT = 12                 (net right shift of 64-bit result)

Inputs: r0=s64 product_lo (64-bit multiplicand low 32), r1=s64 product_hi (high 32),
        r2=s32 factor (32-bit signed multiplier, auto sign-extended).
Returns: r0=s32 ((r0:r1 * sign_ext(r2)) >> 12 truncated to low 32 bits).
Side effects: none.
