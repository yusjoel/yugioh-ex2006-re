enqueue_zone_subentry_sprites_with_xy_split @ 0x0806f2cc

When the tile count in the zone entry matches the count field in slot[4]:
iterates over zone sub-entries, builds a bitmap mask for each valid sub-entry,
then calls enqueue_sprite_attr_with_xy_split to enqueue the sprite attribute
split into X/Y coordinate components. After the loop, also calls
enqueue_sprite_attr_type11 to submit a trailing type-11 record.
Returns 0.

Constants:
  ZONE_TABLE_BASE           = 0x0201c510
  ZONE_TABLE_PLAYER_STRIDE  = 0x868
  ZONE_ENTRY_SIZE           = 0x14
  TILE_COUNT_MATCH_FLAG     = bit2 of slot[4]

Parameters:
  r0: u8* slot_ptr -- equip effect-slot pointer
Return: r0 = 0 (fixed)

Side effects:
  Calls enqueue_sprite_attr_with_xy_split (writes sprite attribute queue,
  X/Y split form) per valid sub-entry.
  Calls enqueue_sprite_attr_type11 (writes type-11 sprite record at end).
