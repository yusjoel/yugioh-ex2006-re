Equip slot eligibility check with full five-layer filter. Guards: (1) slot[+2] bit0 != r1 (opposite-side, bne fail); (2) zone [0..4] calls check_slot_card_is_equip_whitelist; (3) gDuelFieldSlots slot occupied; (4) slot[+8] == 0 (equip valid slot empty); (5) check_zone_slot_equip_prerequisites + check_card_equip_eligible_for_slot(r3=1). Symmetric sibling of 0x080535b0 (side polarity reversed).

Params: r0=u8* equipping_slot (ptr); r1=u8 player_id [0..1]; r2=u8 zone_slot_idx [0..4]
Returns: r0=u32 bool (1=eligible, 0=rejected)
Side effects: none
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868, SLOT_IDX_MAX=4
