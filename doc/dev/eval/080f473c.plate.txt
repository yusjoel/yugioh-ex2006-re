Full puzzle scene init entry with charset flag configuration. Reads EWRAM [0x02000000+0x6c2c] byte, extracts bits[2:0] (lsls#0x1d/lsrs#0x1d) into r4. Sequentially calls init_puzzle_scene_state (full puzzle scene state init including EWRAM zero-fill + three subsystem inits) and write_puzzle_charset_flag(r4) (writes language flag into charset control word). Called for puzzle scene re-entry where charset parameter is needed beyond the base init.
