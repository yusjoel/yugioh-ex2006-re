LP display row type 18 (0x12) single-value wrapper: truncates r1 to low 16 bits, calls set_lp_display_row_fields(r0, 0x12, r1&0xffff, 0) with fixed type=0x12. Symmetric with set_lp_row_type6_with_value/type10_with_value, differs only in type value. Frequently called by multiple LP display paths (0x08069446, 0x08070ae0, 0x0807195a etc, tags=[duel_field]).
Side effects: writes LP display row type=0x12 field via set_lp_display_row_fields.
Constants: ROW_TYPE=0x12 (18, fixed; movs r1,#0x12), MASK=0xffff.
