enqueue_slot_sprite_type4_with_card_id @ 0x08070aa4

Reads card_id from equip effect-slot slot[0] (halfword) and player_id from
slot[2] bit0. Calls enqueue_sprite_attr_type11(player_id, card_id, type=4,
flags=0) to enqueue a type-4 sprite attribute. Sibling of
enqueue_slot_sprite_type11_with_card_id; differs in type parameter (4 vs 0x11)
and in player_id being dynamically extracted from slot[2] bit0.
Always returns 0.

Constants:
  SPRITE_TYPE_4  = 4
  PLAYER_ID_BIT  = bit0 of slot[2]

Parameters:
  r0: u8* slot_ptr -- equip effect-slot pointer
Return: r0 = 0 (fixed)

Side effects:
  Calls enqueue_sprite_attr_type11 (writes one type-4 sprite attribute record
  to the sprite attribute queue).
