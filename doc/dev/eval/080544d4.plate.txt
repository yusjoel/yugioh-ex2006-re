Equip card eligibility predicate verifying target slot card_icid matches equipping card slot[+0] low 16 bits. Guards: (1) compute gDuelFieldSlots[r1&1][r2] target slot; (2) ldrh [slot,#8]!=0 (equip valid flag) -- invalid returns 0; (3) read target slot bits[18:0]=card_icid; (4) ldrh [equip_slot,#0]=equipping card low 16-bit id; compare -- mismatch returns 0. Match returns 1. Semantic: equipping card may only target a zone occupied by the same card identity. No bl calls. Read-only predicate, no side effects.
Inputs: r0=ptr equipping-card slot, r1=u8 player_id [0..1], r2=u8 zone_slot_idx [0..0x14].
Outputs: r0=u32 bool (1=icid matches, 0=mismatch or invalid).
Side effects: none.
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868.
