Advances the cursor/selected position in the pack card list. Reads pack_ui_state+0xc+0x20 (current display card index), calls get_card_passcode_hash to get the passcode hash for the current card. Compares index against total card count (0x095b7cca[0]) to choose the traversal path: if index > total, searches a larger range for a slot matching the passcode and writes to [+0x18]; otherwise searches the local slot list [+0x18..] for an empty slot. On match, writes slot_id and marks bit1 in [+0x1a+slot*2]. On no match, increments [+0x20] and loops (upper bound 0x12b=299). After a match, triggers palette load (load_pack_palette_by_slot) and state field update (pack_ui_state+0x4 := 0xb or 0xf). Called directly by the pack scene; indeg=0 in callgraph.
