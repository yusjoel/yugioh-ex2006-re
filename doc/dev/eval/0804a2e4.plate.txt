Wrapper to enqueue equip slot sprite for zone=0xd (zone 13). r0=player_id, r1=slot_idx, r2=extra_arg. Calls get_zone_slot_ptr(zone=0xd, slot_idx); then calls enqueue_equip_slot_sprite_with_card_check(player_id, 0xd, slot_idx, slot_ptr, extra_arg). Exit: pop {r0}; bx r0 void return. Symmetric counterpart to enqueue_equip_slot_sprite_zone12 (0x0804a30c); only zone differs (0xd vs 0xc). Both called from duel_field 0x08069260. Side effects: OAM sprite attr buffer via enqueue_equip_slot_sprite_with_card_check. Constants: ZONE=0xd.
