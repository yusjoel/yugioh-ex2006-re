Constants: gP1LifePoints=0x0201c4e0, gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868, phase_code_offset=0x1d1c, icid_check=0x137e, player_id_offset=0x1ce8, phase_code_bias_offset=0x1d1c, phase_code_lp_offset=0x011c (bit23 action avail).
