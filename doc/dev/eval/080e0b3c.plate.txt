Pack card list scene main step dispatch function. Dispatched indirectly by dispatch_pack_card_select_substep (0x080e0fb8) via ROM step-table 0x09e495f4. Reads pack_ui_state+0xc low 3 bits for 6-case switch: case0=fadein; case1=jump to card info page; case2=tick card info page; case3=init card list display; case4=dispatch card list scene and reset BG2 affine matrix (BG2X=0xffffc000/BG2Y=0/PA=0x100/PB~PD=0); case5=enable display (DISPCNT := 0x1701) and tick blend to completion, writes step ID := 0x14. Side-effects: DISPCNT 0x04000000, BG2X/Y/PA-PD affine regs, pack_ui_state+0xc+0x0/+0x2, +0x4 step ID. Returns r0=r6 (0=continue/1=done).
