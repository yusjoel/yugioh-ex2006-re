Compact equip slot eligibility predicate. Compared to 0x08051998, omits check_card_equip_eligible_for_slot final call. Three checks: (1) if r2<=4, whitelist check; (2) gDuelFieldSlots[r1&1][r2] slot word bits[18:0] non-zero (slot occupied); (3) extract set_code_a and compare with equipping slot[+4] bits[14:8] -- equal rejects (same set disallowed). Returns 1 on pass. Simplified variant of 0x08051998 for fast set_code screening. Read-only predicate, no side effects.
Inputs: r0=ptr equipping-card slot, r1=u8 player_id [0..1], r2=u8 zone_slot_idx [0..0x14].
Outputs: r0=u32 bool (1=eligible, 0=rejected).
Side effects: none.
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868.
