State 0x80: checks [0x0201e2a0+8+player*4] for value 1; if 1 calls sample_prng_scaled(2) and stores result at [gP1LifePoints+0xea<<5=0x1d40]; otherwise calls invoke_card_display_op_0x31_sub8(0x38).
