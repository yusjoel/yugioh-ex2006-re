enqueue_lp_display_row_clear_for_card_player @ 0x080567a0

Extracts player_id from equip card entry and clears LP display row with row=0xffff.
Reads card_entry byte[+2] bit0 -> player_id (r0);
calls set_lp_display_row_type8(player_id, 0xffff, 1) with clear marker r1=0xffff.
Returns 1 always. Used in equip activation chain to clear LP display row entry
for the specific player.

Constants:
- LP_ROW_CLEAR = 0xffff

Inputs: r0=equip_card_entry ptr (byte[+2] bit0=player_id [0..1])
Output: r0 = 1 (always success)
Side effects: LP display row queue -- set_lp_display_row_type8(player_id, 0xffff, 1) clears row
