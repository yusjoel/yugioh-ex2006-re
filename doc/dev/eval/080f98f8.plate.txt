Iterates over a struct array at r0, counting entries whose bits[1:0] equal 1, and returns the count.
Entry stride is r1 bytes; total entries is r2. Caller (0x080f9944) uses this to count activated-flag entries in a pack scene context.

Constants:
- FLAG_MASK = 0x3 (bits[1:0])
- FLAG_VALUE = 0x1 (target flag value)
