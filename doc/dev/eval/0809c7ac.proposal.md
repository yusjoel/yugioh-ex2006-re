## plate comment (中文, ASCII 标点)
由 duel_field 主调度枢纽 FUN_0809d984 调用. 读取 [gP1LifePoints+0x1d24] 计数器 (<=9 继续); 以 counter/5 得商 (eor 入 player_bit), counter%5 得余数 r3; 计算手牌槽地址 gP1LifePoints + player*0x868 + r3*0x14 + 0x30; 读取槽数据低 13 位作为 card_type_id, 加上 (-0x17d2) 后与 [0..8] 比较进行 9-entry switch 分派. 匹配时: 若槽 [+0x8]!=0 (有装备锁) 则跳过; 否则构建 OAM attr (0xa2<<0x14 prefix + face/orient bits), 调用 apply_equip_activation_with_id_lookup; 命中递增 [gP1LifePoints+0x1cf4] 子计数器并返回 0. 未命中时计数器 +1 重新循环直至 >9 返回 1. Side effects: equip activation state; [gP1LifePoints+0x1cf4] counter. Constants: COUNTER_OFFSET=0x1d24, SLOT_ZONE=hand, CARD_TYPE_BASE=0x17d2, SLOT_STRIDE=0x14, player_stride=0x868, OAM_PREFIX=0xa2<<0x14.
