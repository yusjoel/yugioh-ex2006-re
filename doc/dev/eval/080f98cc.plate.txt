Iterates over bits[0..25] of gUnlockedDuelists and counts the number of set bits, returning the total count of unlocked duelists.
Callers use this count to evaluate unlock progress or compute completion ratios. No external write side-effects; reads only the first word of gUnlockedDuelists.

Constants:
- DUELIST_COUNT = 26 (0x1a+1, bits[0..25], 26 duelist slots)
