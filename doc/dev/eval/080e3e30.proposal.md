Constants:
- EWRAM_PUZZLE_FLAGS = 0x02006c3c (谜题通关标志数组基地址, 每条 4 字节)
- CLEARED_FLAG_MASK = 0x3 (取 bits[1:0])
- CLEARED_VALUE = 0x1 (bit0=1 表示已通关)
