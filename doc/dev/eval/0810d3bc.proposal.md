## plate comment (中文, ASCII 标点)
音效通道音量/pan 双字节写入函数. 入参 r0 为 volume_raw [0..0x7f] (上限). 若 r0 <= 0x3f: 写 r0 到 [base+0x37c] (volume byte), 写 0x3f 到 [base+0x37d] (pan byte); 若 r0 > 0x3f: 写 0x3f 到 [base+0x37c], 写 (0x7f-r0) 到 [base+0x37d]. 实现 volume-pan 联动: 小音量直接写, 大音量时取补值作为 pan 偏移, 形成平移声场效果.

Constants:
ENGINE_BASE=0x030050cc (IWRAM sound engine struct)
VOL_BYTE_OFF=0x37c (0xdf*4, volume byte)
PAN_BYTE_OFF=0x37d (volume/pan secondary byte)
VOL_THRESHOLD=0x3f (split point: below=direct, above=pan-complemented)
PAN_MAX=0x7f (complement base: pan = 0x7f - r0)

## 参数签名
- r0: u8 volume_raw [0..0x7f] - 原始音量值; <=0x3f 直接写, >0x3f 用补值写 pan
- 返回: r0 = void (Pattern B: pop{r0};bx r0 覆盖 r0; 无独立返回值)

## 副作用
- [0x030050cc+0x37c] := min(r0, 0x3f) (volume byte)
- [0x030050cc+0x37d] := 0x3f (if r0<=0x3f) or (0x7f-r0) (if r0>0x3f) (pan byte)
