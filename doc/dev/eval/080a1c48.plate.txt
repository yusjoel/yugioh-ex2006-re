LP display row type 6 single-value wrapper: truncates r1 to low 16 bits (lsls#0x10; lsrs#0x10), calls set_lp_display_row_fields(r0, 6, r1&0xffff, 0) with fixed type=6. Symmetric with set_lp_row_type4_with_value, differs in type value (4 vs 6). Called by equip LP update paths (0x0806ab58, 0x0806c940, 0x0807351e etc, tags=[duel_field]).
Side effects: writes LP display row type=6 field via set_lp_display_row_fields.
Constants: ROW_TYPE=6 (fixed; movs r1,#0x6), MASK=0xffff.
