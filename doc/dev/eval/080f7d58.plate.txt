Copies a row of tile data from a source buffer to a BG VRAM tile slot. r0=src_ptr, r1=tile_index, r2=packed_dims (high 8 bits=tile_count, next 8 bits=row_height). tile_index multiplied by 32 plus BG_VRAM_BASE(0x06010000) gives dst address; packed_dims split into row_height (bits[23:16]) and tile_count (bits[31:24]); calls tile_2d_row_copy for row-by-row copy. Leaf function. indeg=0.

Constants:
- BG_VRAM_BASE = 0x06010000
- TILE_SIZE = 0x20 (32 bytes)
- TILE_SHIFT = 0xb (<<16 >>0xb = *32)
- PACKED_ROW_SHIFT = 0x8 (bits[23:16] = row_height, <<8>>24)
- PACKED_CNT_SHIFT = 0x18 (bits[31:24] = tile_count, >>24)

Inputs: r0=src_ptr (source tile row data pointer), r1=tile_index [0..0x3ff], r2=packed_dims (high8=tile_count, next8=row_height)
Returns: void (pop{r0};bx r0 Sub-case E)

Side effects:
- [BG_VRAM_BASE + tile_index*32 .. ): overwritten with tile row data from src_ptr (row_height*tile_count rows)
