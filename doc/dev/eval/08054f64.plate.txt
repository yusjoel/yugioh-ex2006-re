check_equip_slot_eligible_by_opposite_field8_zero_and_prereqs
Equip slot eligibility predicate requiring opposite side + [+8]==0 (primary equip chain unoccupied) + prerequisites.
Caller: check_zone_slot_equip_prerequisites (0x08036770) during equip target selection.
Checks in order: (1) slot occupied (bits[18:0]!=0);
(2) equip_slot[+2]bit0 != player_id (opposite side check, same side rejected);
(3) [slot+8] == 0 (equip chain head empty); (4) check_zone_slot_equip_prerequisites passes.
Returns 1 if all pass. Pure predicate, no external writes.
Constants: gDuelFieldSlots=0x0201c510, player_stride=0x868
Inputs: r0=ptr equip_card_slot, r1=u8 player_id [0..1], r2=u8 zone_slot_idx (unchecked)
Returns: r0=u32 bool (1=eligible, 0=rejected)
